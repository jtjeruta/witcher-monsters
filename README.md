# Witcher Monster Quests

Monster contracts and contract beasts for **RimWorld**. Take hunt jobs from random world quests or friendly settlements, track beasts through villager rumors, and collect silver, goodwill, or the Law of Surprise.

> **Requires Biotech, Anomaly, and [Witcher Base](../witcher).**

## Monster contracts

Colonists (not just witchers) can take contracts two ways:

- **Random world quest** — a frightened elder's letter describing a nearby attack
- **Settlement caravan gizmo** — **Browse witcher contracts** at friendly non-player settlements

Villagers never name the real monster — only vague panic ("hairy beast", "walking trees", "demons in the mist"). The contract site reveals a massacre scene (abandoned cart, ruined camp, or corpse field) and the actual threat.

**Rewards:** silver payment, faction goodwill, or the **Law of Surprise** — a mysterious debt that arrives within a week (precious metals, a farm animal, or a child left in your care).

Optional **Dark Ages** encounter pools load automatically when the Van.Beasts mod is enabled. Optional **VRE-Sanguophage** encounter pools load when Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage is enabled.

## Leshen

The first custom contract beast is the **leshen** — a dormant forest spirit that looks like an ordinary tree until you draw near:

- **Dormant** — stands or shuffles listlessly; ignores distant colonists
- **Vine whip** — ranged cutting attack once awakened
- **Summon wolves** — calls maddened timber wolves to join the hunt
- **Forest blink** — teleports away when foes get too close

Contract beasts cannot be tamed (including via Axii) or calmed with ideology abilities once activated.

## Dev testing

Enable **dev mode**, open the debug actions menu, and use **Witcher Monsters → Spawn Leshen (contract beast)** to spawn one at the map edge.

For contracts, visit a friendly settlement with a caravan or wait for the random world quest offer.

## Compatibility

- Built for **RimWorld 1.6**.
- Requires **Biotech**, **Anomaly**, and **Witcher Base**.
- Optional: **Van.Beasts** for extra contract encounter pools.
- Optional: **Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage** for extra vampire-variant contract pools.
- Requires **Witcher Traditions** research (Witcher Base) before random offers or settlement contract gizmos appear.
- Safe to add mid-save.
